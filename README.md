## What is UniFinance?

**UNIFINANCE** is an educational digital platform designed to support financial education
courses, offering students a simulated environment for learning the dynamics of the stock
market.
The application was created with the **goal of translating theoretical finance concepts
into operational practice**, allowing users to analyze the impact of their investment decisions
within an ecosystem completely free of real financial risk.

## Key Features

### 📈 Live Market Data
Stock prices update daily using real market data. Every decision your students make is based on what's actually happening in the world's financial markets.

### 💼 Virtual Wallet
Each student gets a virtual wallet loaded with imaginary capital imposed by the teacher. They can buy and sell stocks freely — no real money, real consequences.

### 🏆 Weekly Rankings
Every week, UniFinance calculates who performed best — both within each class and globally across the platform. The leaderboard resets weekly, keeping competition fresh.

### 💬 Teacher and student Chat
Teachers and students stay connected through a built-in messaging system.

### 🔐 Secure Login
Sign in with your **Google** or **Microsoft** account — or create a local account directly on the platform. No complicated setup required.

---

## How it works

```
1. Teacher creates a class and invites students
2. Each student receives a virtual wallet with starting budget
3. Students browse the market and invest in real stocks
4. Prices update daily — wallet's gain and lose value in real time
5. Every week, rankings are calculated and published
6. Teachers review performance and guide students toward better decisions
```
